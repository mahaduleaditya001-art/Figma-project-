Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (1931 - 2015)

1931
Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu

A boy on an island

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram.

Father: boat owner and imam. Mother: Ashiamma.

Remember: The family was not rich, but full of good values.

His birthplace in Rameswaram

1940s
Rameswaram

Newspapers and birds

As a boy he delivered newspapers to help his family.

A teacher showed him how birds fly. He decided he would fly too.

Remember: His dream of flying began at the seashore.

Pamban Bridge links Rameswaram island to the mainland

1954
Tiruchirappalli and Chennai

Learning beyond the island

Graduated in Physics from St. Joseph’s College, Tiruchirappalli.

Then studied Aeronautical Engineering at MIT, Chennai.

Remember: Changing your path is not failure.

St. Joseph’s College, Tiruchirappalli

9th of 8
Indian Air Force selection

The missed dream

He wanted to be a fighter pilot, but came 9th when only 8 seats were available.

He then joined DRDO as a scientist.

Remember: The pilot who missed the cockpit later built rockets and missiles.
✈️
1969
ISRO, Sriharikota

India’s first launch vehicle

Led the SLV-III project at ISRO.

In July 1980 it put the Rohini satellite in orbit.

Remember: The first flight in 1979 did not fully succeed. The team tried again and won.

Models of ISRO launch vehicles

1982
DRDO, Hyderabad

The Missile Man

Led India’s guided missile programme at DRDO.

Missiles developed: Agni and Prithvi.

Remember: He always said he was one member of a big team.

Agni V launch from Dr. Abdul Kalam Island, Odisha (2018)

2002
Rashtrapati Bhavan, New Delhi

The People’s President

He was the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

He loved meeting students and answering their questions.

Remember: People called him the “People’s President”.

Republic Day eve address, 2007

2007+
Across India

Always a teacher

After the presidency he went back to teaching.

He spoke to students in colleges and schools all over India.

Remember: Even as President, he called himself a teacher first.

With students at IIT Guwahati

2015
IIM Shillong

The last lecture

On 27 July 2015, aged 83, he passed away while teaching at IIM Shillong.

He was laid to rest in his hometown, Rameswaram.

Remember: A life that began on an island shore ended in a classroom.

India Post stamp, 2015

Beyond Science

Kalam played the veena, loved music and loved young minds.

Kalam’s veena at the Rashtrapati Bhavan museum

Music: He played the veena.
Books: He wrote many books for young readers.
Students: He believed the future of India is in its classrooms.
